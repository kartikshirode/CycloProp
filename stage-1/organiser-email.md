## If you write anyway

There is a reasonable case for one short mail, not to unblock anything but to put the team on the organisers' radar early and to confirm the submission format, which the problem statement never specifies. It says every stage is handled by email and names no page limit, file format or naming convention.

Worth asking, in one paragraph:

- Is there a preferred format or page limit for the Stage 1 preliminary design submission, and a naming convention for the attachment?
- Should the registered team ID or registration reference appear anywhere in the submission?

Both are cheap questions with a real answer, unlike the three above, and getting the format right costs nothing.

Send it after registering, from the registered address, with the team name and lead in the signature. Do not send anything to the organisers automatically; that is a blocked trigger in [../.codex/weekly-loop.md](../.codex/weekly-loop.md) and stays a human action.
