Ready to send once the team is registered and named. One mail, three questions. Question 1 gates the sizing work, so this should go out early in week 1 rather than late.

**To:** pushpak_gc2026@aero.iitb.ac.in
**Subject:** CycloProp Stage 1: clarification on thrust-to-weight basis and problem statement scope

---

Dear PUSHPAK Grand Challenge team,

We are preparing a Stage 1 submission for the CycloProp advanced UAV propulsion challenge and have three questions we would like to settle before we fix our design targets.

**1. Basis of the thrust-to-weight requirement.** The competition page asks for a cycloidal rotor module producing at least 10 N of thrust with a thrust-to-weight ratio above 2.5. We would like to confirm what the weight in that ratio refers to. If it is the rotor module alone, then 10 N at T/W of 2.5 puts the module under roughly 408 g including blades, hub, pitch mechanism, motor and supporting structure. If it is the complete aircraft, the module has to be considerably lighter, since the same 10 N would also be carrying the airframe, battery, avionics and payload. The two readings lead to materially different designs, so we would rather ask than assume.

**2. The second objective.** The FAQ on the CycloProp page answers a question about two objectives by stating that only one objective is given, and it carries an editorial note about confirming the second with the team before publication. That note is still live on the page. Could you tell us what the second objective is, or confirm that there is only one?

**3. Detailed problem statement.** We have not found a problem statement document for CycloProp beyond the competition page itself. Could you confirm whether a fuller specification exists, and if so where to obtain it? We want to be sure we are not working from a partial brief.

Thank you for your time. We are happy to be pointed at published material rather than given direct answers if that is more appropriate.

Regards,
